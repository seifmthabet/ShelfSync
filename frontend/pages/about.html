About ShelfSync

ShelfSync connects readers with a curated catalog of books. Members can search the collection, open detailed records for each title, request to borrow physical or tracked copies, and share reviews once librarians approve them for the public archive.

Who it is for

Students, researchers, and leisure readers who want a single place to discover books, follow their loans, and keep a simple reading history.

Librarians

Staff accounts can moderate borrow requests, manage the catalog, review user submissions, and oversee member accounts from the admin dashboard.
