How ShelfSync works

1. Create an account

Sign up with your email, then sign in to access borrowing, reviews, and your personal dashboard.

2. Find a book

Use Browse to filter and search the catalog. Open any title to read its description, ratings, and approved reviews.

3. Request a borrow

From the book page, submit a borrow request. A librarian reviews it; you will see updates under your dashboard and borrowed items.

4. Write a review

Signed-in members can submit reviews. They may be moderated before appearing publicly on the book page.
